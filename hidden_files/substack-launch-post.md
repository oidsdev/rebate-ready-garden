# Substack post — Rebate-Ready Garden launch (2026-10-01)

## Title
Your county might pay you to plant a rain garden

## Body
Most homeowners have never heard of this, so I'll just say it plainly: your county might pay you to tear out lawn and plant a rain garden.

I spent the last few weeks building a free database of stormwater rebate programs — 23 of them so far, every one verified against the actual program rules. Prince George's County pays $10 per square foot for rain gardens. Montgomery County has Rainscapes. DC has RiverSmart. The list goes on, and almost nobody knows any of this exists.

Why would a county pay you for this? The rain that sheets off roofs and lawns every storm is one of the biggest sources of water pollution. A rain garden catches it and lets it soak in instead. Counties figured out it's cheaper to pay homeowners than to build bigger storm drains.

That's the whole reason I built this. I kept running into the same gap: the money is real, the programs are real, and the only people who know about them are the ones already deep in the native-plant world.

The database is free and always will be. For each program it shows what it pays for, who qualifies, and how to apply. No account, no email capture, just the information.

If you want the easy button, I also made planting plans matched to each program's rules — $19.50 each, sized to the rebate math so you're not guessing. Totally optional. The free database is the point.
