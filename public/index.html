Rebate money, explained

Did you know your county may pay you to plant a rain garden?

Rebate programs across Maryland and DC pay homeowners hundreds or thousands of dollars to swap lawn for native plantings — but the rules, plant lists, and paperwork stop most people before they start. Each $19.50 plan turns your county’s program requirements into a ready-to-plant layout: the Plant-by-Numbers map, the native plant list, and the compliance packet, as an instant download.
