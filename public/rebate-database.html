23 stormwater rebate programs, checked September 30, 2026

Rebate programs pay homeowners to manage stormwater where it falls — usually with rain gardens, conservation landscaping, or native plantings that soak up runoff instead of sending it into sewers and streams. There is no national program: every rebate is run by a county, city, water utility, or watershed group, so the rules differ state to state and even town to town. Some pay a flat rate per square foot, others reimburse a percentage of your costs, and a few work as tax credits or discounts on your stormwater fee. Some require an approved plan before you dig; others let you do the work yourself and send in receipts. Funding opens and closes, so amounts and deadlines move. This page is our free, working directory of every residential program we could verify — checked September 30, 2026 — so you can find yours and confirm the current terms with the official source before you apply.
