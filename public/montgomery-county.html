The plans for this program

Five plans, each with the to-scale layout, the native plant list with sizes, quantities, and spacing, the RainScapes compliance packet, and the submission sheet. $39 each.

How it works

  1. Pick the plan that matches your yard: sun or shade, standard or small lot.
  2. Check out. The download link arrives by email and stays valid 72 hours.
  3. Adapt the layout to your property, complete the county site assessment, and submit.

Approval is the county's decision. Your application will be complete.
